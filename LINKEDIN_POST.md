🔍 3 Surprising Data Insights:

1️⃣ The Tale of Two Diasporas (NRI vs PIO):
Mauritius (865k Indians) & Fiji (316k) have 60-70% Indian-origin populations, yet send minimal remittances. Why? 99% are 4th-gen PIO citizens with local assets. In contrast, the UAE (4.3M) and Saudi Arabia (2.7M) are 99% NRIs on temporary visas who remit billions home to support families.

2️⃣ Where the Money Lands in India (All 28 States & 8 UTs):
Calibrated against official RBI remittance surveys:
• Maharashtra (~35.2%): Global financial hub & high-value NRE deposits
• Kerala (~10.2%): The traditional Gulf workforce consumption lifeline
• Tamil Nadu (~8.8%): Singapore, Malaysia & Gulf professional corridors
• Karnataka (~7.6%): US Silicon Valley & STEM tech deposits
• Gujarat & Punjab (~5.4% - 5.8%): US, UK & Canada diaspora
• UP & Bihar (~6.7%): Rapidly accelerating Gulf blue-collar corridors

3️⃣ The "Rupee Windfall" Effect:
When the Rupee weakens against the Dollar, an NRI sends home more Rupees with zero extra labor. A shift from ₹86.5 to ₹90/$ injects over ₹44,000 Crore (~$5.2B) into Indian household savings.
